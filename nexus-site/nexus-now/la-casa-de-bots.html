01 THE WORKSHOP

La Casa De Bots was a NEXUS Robotics workshop built around one goal: take a robot from bare sensors to a machine that finds its own way out of a maze.

Sessions were designed and delivered by NEXUS team members, walking participants through the maze-solver problem end to end — sensing, decision logic and motion — with robots on the table throughout.

02 THE BUILD PATH

SENSE

Reading walls and lines with sensor arrays — the raw inputs every maze decision depends on.

DECIDE

Turning sensor readings into navigation logic — the rule set that guides the bot through junctions and dead ends.
